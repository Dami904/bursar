Agents work while you're away. Alerts bring the moments that need a person, like a payment to approve or a job that looks wrong, to wherever you are: a Telegram chat, your own server, or both.

## What you're alerted about

| Alert | When it fires | Type |
|---|---|---|
| **A payment needs your approval** | A payment is above the job's threshold and waiting for you. One alert per payment. | `needs_approval` |
| **A payment is stuck** | A payment has been in flight for more than 5 minutes without settling or being refunded. Bursar keeps checking the chain meanwhile. | `stuck_payment` |
| **Budget 80% used** | Settled, held, waiting and stuck payments together reach 80% of the job's budget. Once per job. | `budget_80` |
| **A job was frozen** | The vault paid out money Bursar can't match to any of the job's payments. The job is frozen at once and paused on-chain. See [Security model](/docs/security). | `job_frozen` |
| **Many requests blocked** | 3 or more payments refused on one job within 10 minutes: often an agent that's confused, or being pushed by something it read. | `denial_burst` |

Every alert has a title, a short description and a link straight to the right place in the console: the Approvals page, or the job.

## Telegram

1. In the console, open **Alerts** (the bell) and choose **Connect Telegram**.
2. Telegram opens a chat with the Bursar bot, with a one-time link code already filled in. Press **Start**.
3. The bot confirms, and the chat appears under Alerts as "Telegram · Chat linked".

The link code works once and expires after an hour. One Telegram chat is linked per Bursar account; to move alerts to a different chat, remove the linked one first, then connect again.

Messages look like this:

```text
0.15 USDC needs your approval
Short film: Money for machines: "The closing scene needs the market report…"
https://<console>/app/approvals
```

## Webhooks

Webhooks send each alert to your own server as a signed JSON `POST`, so you can page someone, post to Slack, open a ticket, or have another agent react.

1. Under **Alerts**, enter your endpoint's URL and choose **Add webhook**.
2. Bursar shows a **signing secret** once. Store it on your server.
3. Choose **Send a test alert** to check everything is wired up.

Your endpoint must be a public `https` (or `http`) address. Bursar refuses private and local addresses, doesn't follow redirects, and waits at most 5 seconds for an answer. Answer with any `2xx` status to confirm delivery. The payload, headers and signature check are in the [Webhooks reference](/docs/webhooks).

## Delivery and retries

Alerts go through an outbox, so a restart or a network blip doesn't lose them:

- Each event is queued **once**. Bursar won't send the same approval request twice.
- If a target fails, the alert is retried after 1 minute, then 2, 4, 8 and so on (never more than an hour apart), up to **8 attempts**.
- An alert counts as delivered only when every target accepted it.

<Callout>

An alert queued while you had no targets set up isn't sent later when you add one. Set up alerts before handing an agent real work, and use **Send a test alert** to be sure.

</Callout>
