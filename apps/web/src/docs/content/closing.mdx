You're always one wallet confirmation away from stopping a job, and one more from having its money back. This guide covers pausing, resuming, closing, expiry, and what happens to money from failed payments.

All of these are in the job page's **⋯** menu. Each runs from **your wallet**, on the vault itself, so none of it depends on Bursar's server behaving.

## Pause

**Pause job** stops every payment at once. The vault refuses all releases while a job is paused, whatever Bursar's server asks, so nothing leaves until you resume it. Agents' requests are refused with `JOB_NOT_ACTIVE`.

Use it when an agent is doing something you don't understand, when a key may have leaked, or just to think.

- Payments already sent before the pause finish normally.
- Bursar's server can pause a job too, in an emergency. It does this automatically when it sees an unexplained payout. It can never resume one: only the owner can.

## Resume

**Resume job** lets payments flow again, under the same rules.

Pausing and resuming both count as a change of rules on-chain, so any approval signed **before** a pause is void afterwards. Payments that were waiting for approval need approving again.

## Close and get the money back

**Close job** ends the job for good and returns every unspent USDC to your wallet in the same transaction. The menu shows the exact amount: "Close and return 0.57 USDC".

The console lets you close only when nothing is **held, waiting or stuck**:

- **Waiting for approval**: approve or reject those payments first.
- **Held** (being paid right now): wait a few seconds for them to finish.
- **Stuck**: wait until Bursar settles them or refunds them (see below).

This makes sure every payment is accounted for before the books close. After closing, the job is read-only: its history, evidence and audit trail stay available, but no more agents, keys or payments.

## When a job expires

At the expiry date the job stops paying anyone. Bursar refuses with `JOB_EXPIRED`, and the vault refuses too, by chain time. The money stays in the vault until you close the job; nothing is returned automatically. Close it from the menu as above.

## Money from failed payments

Sometimes the vault has released money for a purchase but the seller never settles it: the seller is down, refuses the payment, or answers too late. Bursar doesn't guess. It waits until the signed payment has **expired by chain time** (so it can no longer be settled), then sends the money back from the job's payment wallet to the vault and credits the job. The budget bar shows these as **stuck** until they're resolved. You get an alert if one takes longer than 5 minutes.

Every refund is checked on-chain: the vault only credits USDC that has actually arrived, never more than was released for that payment, and never twice.

## Frozen jobs

If the vault ever pays out money that Bursar can't match to one of the job's payments, for example because someone used a leaked server key outside Bursar, the job is **frozen** at once, paused on-chain, and you get an alert. Check the job's transactions on the explorer. Once you're satisfied, choose **Unfreeze**: it clears the freeze in Bursar and resumes the job on-chain from your wallet. See [Security model](/docs/security).
